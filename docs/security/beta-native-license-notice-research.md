## NOTICE rule and unresolved decision

[Apache-2.0 §4](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) describes redistribution conditions: supply the license; mark modified files; retain pertinent source-form notices; and, **if the Work includes a `NOTICE` text file as part of its distribution**, carry its pertinent attribution notices in a permitted readable location. The condition is not a universal instruction that every Apache-licensed dependency has a `NOTICE` file. The same source says a `NOTICE` is informational and does not modify the license. The candidate now carries the [pinned AWS repository `NOTICE`](https://github.com/aws/aws-lc-rs/blob/22e629d5c46276497a24ee3e575be4315940e7cb/NOTICE), but whether other package-level or source-file notices must appear in Arany's particular binary/source distributions still requires a review of actual selected files and release artifacts. Cargo license fields and the present Arany `NOTICE` alone cannot resolve that question. [AWS crate source listing](https://docs.rs/crate/aws-lc-rs/1.18.1/source/).

The [pinned sys-crate `LICENSE`](https://docs.rs/crate/aws-lc-sys/0.45.0/source/LICENSE) also states, in its BSD-3-Clause text, that binary redistributions reproduce the copyright notice, conditions, and disclaimer in accompanying documentation or other materials. Its MIT and ISC texts likewise contain notice-retention conditions. These are source-text observations, not a determination of the obligations or chosen alternative for a future Arany distribution.
