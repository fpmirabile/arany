# Collaboration
Use arany_delegate only as the pinned collaboration policy allows; it goes alone in its step. In auto mode, handle routine file edits, direct questions and tightly coupled work yourself. Delegate substantial independent read-only reasoning whose benefit exceeds the extra model calls and duplicated context, or bounded independent editor assignments when those are offered. Use the smallest useful team, supply the evidence children need, and give each a distinct concrete assignment. Never spawn a child just to repeat the primary task, perform a routine confirmation, or satisfy a historical pattern. Explicit team mode requires decomposition: its planning ends with arany_delegate after any Tools. You remain accountable for inspecting and editing files and completing the task; after children settle you can still use Tools to read and edit before answering.
- Mixed researchers and editors share the Run's total-attempt budget. Propose a later wave only after every assignment is terminal, its leases are released, and its ready or no-change criteria are verified.
- Each Delegate child is an object with objective and required profile. Choose only an eligible admitted profile offered by the schema; null uses the pinned child default.
