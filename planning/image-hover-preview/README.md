# Image chip hover preview

## Metadata

- Status: `Ready` (Research and Plan gates passed; implementation awaits image attachment chips, [PR #425](https://github.com/fpmirabile/arany/pull/425))
- Owner: Fernando Mirabile
- Last updated: 2026-10-25

## Outcome and scope

One feature: in Fullscreen with mouse capture, moving the pointer over an `[Image #N]` composer chip shows a floating balloon with that image, drawn through the terminal's own graphics protocol; moving the pointer off the chip removes it. Terminals without a detected protocol, Classic, linear and screen-reader modes, `ARANY_DISABLE_MOUSE`, and tmux show nothing: no half-block, braille or text fallback. The harness responsibility served is the attached terminal client ([system overview §8](../../docs/architecture/system-overview.md#8-terminal-presentation)); the Engine, journal, Provider context and Tools are untouched.

Excluded: previews of images in History or Tool results, clicking or zooming the balloon, graphics in Classic or linear output, and any terminal configuration change (such as tmux `allow-passthrough`). Claude Code and Codex CLI have no image hover preview; this is an Arany addition with no peer wire format to copy, so the protocol facts below come from the protocol owners and from ratatui-image as the closest Rust peer.

## Research

Chips and attachments ([PR #425](https://github.com/fpmirabile/arany/pull/425), the prerequisite):

- `Composer` holds `images: Vec<ImageAttachment>` and `selected_image: Option<usize>`; `draw_composer` ([view.rs](../../crates/arany-cli/src/terminal/view.rs)) renders the chips as `[Image #N]` spans inside the composer header row (`composer_area.y`), after the `Ask Arany` title, or one compact chip when they do not fit. The header row is the only place a chip occupies cells.
- `ImageAttachment` ([image.rs](../../crates/arany/src/provider/image.rs)) stores the admitted PNG as base64 (`base64()`), its byte length, `dimensions()` from IHDR, a digest and `description(index)` (`Image N: PNG WxH, N bytes`). `MAX_IMAGE_BYTES` is 192 KiB and `MAX_MESSAGE_IMAGES` is four. The spec keeps PNG bytes undecoded: "Admitted PNG bytes are kept unchanged, including metadata, without local decompression" ([paste and attachments](../../docs/specs/terminal.md#paste-and-attachments)). No PNG decoder, inflater or image crate is in `Cargo.lock` (checked for `png`, `image`, the inflate crates and `crc32fast`).

Pointer input (current `main`):

- Fullscreen enables `?1000h ?1002h ?1006h` only; the constant comment and the spec say any-motion `?1003` stays off ([terminal.rs `MOUSE_CAPTURE`](../../crates/arany-cli/src/terminal.rs), [selectors](../../docs/specs/terminal.md#selectors)). Without `?1003` an emulator reports motion only while a button is held, so hover is unobservable today.
- The raw decoder ([input/raw.rs](../../crates/arany-cli/src/terminal/input/raw.rs)) already parses SGR motion records (`button & 32` with buttons `3`) into `MouseEventKind::Moved`, mapped to `TerminalInput::PointerMove { column, row }`; `AttachedTerminal::ignores_pointer` discards every `PointerMove` without a redraw. Records are bounded by `MAX_MOUSE_RECORD_BYTES` (32), the reader outbox by `OUTBOX_LIMIT` (256) plus `MAX_BACKLOG`, and the channel by `INPUT_QUEUE_CAPACITY` (32); an over-full backlog is an error, never silent loss of keys.
- Frame geometry is recorded after each draw: `history_area`, `agent_picker_area` and `panel_selection.area` are set by the drawing path and consumed by pointer hit tests (`history_cell`, `agent_picker_index`, `panel_pointer_input`). Chip rectangles have no owner yet.
- `TerminalQueries` ([input.rs](../../crates/arany-cli/src/terminal/input.rs)) sends `KEYBOARD_QUERY` (`CSI ? u` then DA1 `CSI c`) on Fullscreen acquisition and waits `KEYBOARD_PROBE_TIMEOUT` (250 ms) for `KeyboardReply::Flags` or `KeyboardReply::DeviceAttributes`; replies bypass the full event queue. The decoder drains OSC, DCS and APC strings in `Mode::ControlString` to their terminator and discards them, bounded by `RECORD_DEADLINE`.
- Draws go through `TerminalBackend` over `CrosstermBackend<TerminalOutput>` on stderr ([backend.rs](../../crates/arany-cli/src/terminal/backend.rs)); `draw_frame` resets panel-selection geometry and calls `terminal.draw`. ratatui 0.30.2 (`ratatui-core` 0.1.2) offers `Cell::set_diff_option(CellDiffOption::Skip)`, documented for cells "covered by something from an escape sequence, such as graphics", and `CellDiffOption::Always`.
- Headless frames are asserted with `TestBackend` in the [frame corpus](../../crates/arany-cli/src/terminal/view/tests.rs); the raw decoder has reply and mouse tables in its own module; PTY journeys live in [chat_mouse.rs](../../crates/arany-cli/tests/session_run/chat_mouse.rs) and [active_terminal.rs](../../crates/arany-cli/tests/session_run/active_terminal.rs).

Protocols (read from their owners on 2026-10-25):

- [Kitty graphics protocol](https://sw.kovidgoyal.net/kitty/graphics-protocol/): detection is `ESC _ G i=31,s=1,v=1,a=q,t=d,f=24;AAAA ESC \` followed by DA1 `CSI c`; a supporting terminal answers `ESC _ G i=31;OK ESC \` before the DA1 reply, a non-supporting one answers DA1 alone. `f=100` transmits PNG bytes that the terminal decodes itself. Data is base64 in chunks of at most 4096 bytes, `m=1` on every chunk but the last. `c=` and `r=` scale the image into that many cells, letterboxed to keep the aspect ratio. `a=d,d=I,i=ID` deletes a placement and frees the data; `q=2` suppresses every response; `C=1` leaves the cursor in place. Unicode-placeholder mode (`U=1`) exists for tmux and other hosts, and requires passthrough on the host side.
- [iTerm2 inline images](https://iterm2.com/documentation-images.html): `OSC 1337 ; File=inline=1;width=N;height=M;preserveAspectRatio=1:BASE64 ST`, where `N`/`M` are cell counts; the terminal decodes PNG itself; 1,048,576-byte limit; no deletion command (an image is attached to its cells and disappears when they are rewritten); old tmux limits the whole sequence to 256 bytes.
- Sixel has no PNG transport: the client decodes, scales and quantizes to at most 256 colors, and the output is pixel-exact, so the cell pixel size must be known (`CSI 16 t` or `TIOCGWINSZ` `ws_xpixel`/`ws_ypixel`; `rustix::termios::tcgetwinsize` is already a dependency of the binary). DA1 attribute `4` advertises Sixel.
- [ratatui-image `picker.rs`](https://github.com/benjajaja/ratatui-image/blob/master/src/picker.rs) (closest Rust peer): one combined stdout query (Kitty `a=q`, DA1, `CSI 16 t`, `CSI 1337 n`, `CSI 5 n` as end marker) read under a timeout; Kitty wins over Sixel; iTerm2 is chosen only from `TERM_PROGRAM` (`iTerm`, `WezTerm`, `vscode`, `rio`, `WarpTerminal`) or `LC_TERMINAL`; `WEZTERM_EXECUTABLE`/`KONSOLE_VERSION` blacklist Kitty and Sixel; inside tmux it runs `tmux set -p allow-passthrough on`; no reply means its half-block fallback. Its [Kitty protocol](https://github.com/benjajaja/ratatui-image/blob/master/src/protocol/kitty.rs) transmits raw RGBA (`f=32`), never deletes and relies on placeholders. Arany diverges on the last three points: it sends the admitted PNG as is (no decoder), deletes explicitly on leave, and never changes host configuration ([keyboard mode rule](../../docs/specs/terminal.md#draft-editing-and-submission): "No ... host terminal configuration is enabled automatically").

Dependency policy: workspace dependencies are pinned in [Cargo.toml](../../Cargo.toml); [deny.toml](../../deny.toml) restricts licenses and sources; the [release-policy workflow](../../.github/workflows/release-policy.yml) runs a fresh advisory and dependency-policy scan on every PR; a new dependency is a [security-pass trigger](../../agents/security.md#security-pass-triggers) and, by repository practice, an owner approval recorded in the plan ([real-scale snapshot plan](../real-scale-command-snapshot/README.md)).

## Contract and scenarios

- Owning spec: [terminal.md](../../docs/specs/terminal.md). Changes: (1) [Selectors](../../docs/specs/terminal.md#selectors): Fullscreen adds `?1003` only while a graphics protocol was detected and mouse capture is on, and lists it among the modes every exit path disables; (2) [Paste and attachments](../../docs/specs/terminal.md#paste-and-attachments): a new `Image hover preview` paragraph with detection, balloon geometry, enter/leave, cleanup and the no-fallback rule; (3) [Public limits](../../docs/specs/terminal.md#public-limits-and-compatibility): one `Image hover preview` row (probe timeout, balloon cells, bytes per hover); (4) [Acceptance scenarios](../../docs/specs/terminal.md#acceptance-scenarios-and-evidence-owners): one row naming the owners below. Facts live there once; this plan is deleted at closeout.
- Successful path: Fullscreen on a Kitty-protocol terminal, mouse capture on, a draft with two chips. The pointer enters `[Image #2]`: one redraw places a bordered balloon above the composer header, titled with the chip's existing description, and the PNG is transmitted and placed inside it. The pointer moves within the chip: no redraw, no retransmission. The pointer leaves: the image is deleted, the balloon cells are repainted with the history beneath, one redraw.
- Rejection and failure: no `OK` reply before DA1 (or a timed-out probe) means no `?1003`, no hover state and no output; `ARANY_DISABLE_MOUSE`, Classic, linear, screen-reader and `TMUX` set mean the probe is not sent; a balloon that cannot fit at least `PREVIEW_MIN_ROWS` above the composer is not shown; a chip hidden by the compact header has no rectangle and no hover; a write error while emitting graphics propagates like any render error.
- Recovery and cleanup: resize, opening a picker, dialog, pager or detail view, a chip removal or renumbering, a mode switch, scrollback dump (`[`), suspension, release and exit clear the hover state, delete the placement and (for exits) pop `?1003` with the other pointer modes.
- Evidence owners: [raw decoder tables](../../crates/arany-cli/src/terminal/input/raw.rs) (probe replies, motion records), [frame corpus](../../crates/arany-cli/src/terminal/view/tests.rs) (chip rectangles, balloon placement and clipping, skip cells, repaint after leave), [terminal lifecycle tests](../../crates/arany-cli/src/terminal.rs) (hover transitions, mode sequences), [chat mouse PTY journey](../../crates/arany-cli/tests/session_run/chat_mouse.rs) (any-motion enable/disable bytes and a scripted `OK` reply through a real PTY). Deferred: visual confirmation on kitty, Ghostty, WezTerm, iTerm2 and foot is user-owned dogfooding recorded in [NEXT_STEPS.md](../../NEXT_STEPS.md); Linux evidence stands for macOS per the terminal spec's existing stance, with iTerm2 the macOS-only check.

## Chosen direction

- Simplest sufficient solution after Ponytail: no decoder, no cache, no thread. Kitty and iTerm2 accept the already base64-encoded PNG, so a hover writes `ImageAttachment::base64()` in 4096-character chunks (Kitty) or one OSC (iTerm2) and lets the terminal decode and scale (`c`/`r` or `width`/`height` in cells with aspect preserved). At most one balloon exists, so one fixed Kitty image id suffices and is deleted before reuse; retransmitting at most 256 KiB on each enter is cheaper than owning a cache. The probe rides the existing keyboard probe: `KEYBOARD_QUERY` becomes Kitty query + `CSI ? u` + DA1, one write and one timeout, and the decoder's `ControlString` mode yields a bounded `GraphicsReply` for `ESC _ G i=31;OK`; DA1 parameters are retained so attribute `4` can select Sixel in S3.
- Where it lives: `terminal.rs` owns `GraphicsProtocol` (closed enum `Kitty | ITerm2 | Sixel`, `Option` when absent), the hover state (`Option<usize>`), the chip rectangles returned by `draw_frame_with_history` beside `history_area`, and the post-frame emission through the backend writer; `terminal/view.rs` draws the balloon frame and marks its interior `CellDiffOption::Skip` with `Modifier::HIDDEN`, so leaving (plain history cells) always differs from the previous buffer and repaints without a full clear; `terminal/graphics.rs` (new, `pub(super)`) builds the protocol bytes from `(protocol, &ImageAttachment, inner Rect)` and the delete sequence, pure functions with byte-exact tests. This is house idiom 5 (flow `terminal.rs` plus one phase child) and idiom 6 (the binary maps the closed enum to bytes at the edge). No seam: one adapter per protocol inside one module, no trait.
- Any-motion tracking: `pointer_modes` acquires `MOUSE_CAPTURE_WITH_MOTION` (`?1000h ?1002h ?1003h ?1006h`) only when a protocol was detected, otherwise today's set; `ignores_pointer` keeps discarding `PointerMove` unless it changes the hovered chip, so idle motion costs one hit test and no frame. crossterm's own `EnableMouseCapture` enables `?1003` unconditionally, so emulators handle the rate; Arany keeps it opt-in by capability to stay the cheaper peer.
- Balloon geometry: inner `PREVIEW_COLUMNS` 32 × `PREVIEW_ROWS` 10 cells, shrunk to fit above the header and to the image's aspect ratio assuming 2:1 cell height; border plus a title from `ImageAttachment::description` with the byte count through the shared size formatter ([measures](../../docs/specs/terminal.md#human-readable-measures)); left edge at the chip's first column, clamped to the frame; not shown below `PREVIEW_MIN_ROWS` 4 inner rows. Every number is one constant in `terminal/graphics.rs` and the spec row derives from it (idiom 4).
- Linux/macOS impact: none in code; the protocol bytes are identical. Native evidence: Linux PTY journey for the control bytes; emulator rendering is deferred dogfooding; iTerm2 is the macOS-only emulator and S2's deferred check.
- Alternatives considered: ratatui-image as a dependency (brings `image`, a decoder and raw RGBA transmission that Arany's undecoded-PNG rule forbids); unicode-placeholder placement (needed only for tmux, which is excluded); a half-block fallback (owner excluded it); probing `CSI 16 t` for cell pixels (needed only by Sixel, so S3 reads `tcgetwinsize` first and probes only if it reports zero pixels).

## Security and resource model

Triggered: rendering user-controlled content, a new terminal query path and, in S3, a new parsing dependency.

- Assets and boundaries: the PNG bytes come from the clipboard or an admitted Workspace file and are already untrusted data under PR #425's admission; Arany never decodes them in S1/S2 and emits them only as base64 (`[A-Za-z0-9+/=]`), so no control byte can be injected into the sequence. The terminal's decoder is the terminal's attack surface; this is an explicit non-claim and stays as documented in the spec ("Dimension admission is not raster decoding").
- Terminal replies are untrusted input: the `OK` reply is matched byte-exactly against `i=31;OK` within a `MAX_GRAPHICS_REPLY_BYTES` 32 bound, DA1 parameters are digits only (existing check), anything else is discarded as today. `TERM_PROGRAM`, `LC_TERMINAL` and `TMUX` are the user's environment, as in the existing clipboard owner.
- Authority: the preview grants nothing, reaches no Event, Provider request, diagnostic or telemetry record, and never changes host or tmux configuration.
- Bounds: one probe per acquisition under the existing 250 ms timeout; one balloon; one image id; at most `MAX_ENCODED_IMAGE_BYTES` (256 KiB) plus chunk headers written per enter; motion input bounded by the reader's existing record, outbox and queue limits; S3's decode output bounded by `PREVIEW_MAX_PIXELS` derived from the balloon cells and cell pixel size, with the decoder's own limits set to the admitted PNG byte cap.
- Failure containment: write errors propagate to restoration; every exit path already pops pointer modes, `?1003` joins the same guard; a late S3 decode result for a chip no longer hovered is dropped.

## Risks and mitigations

- `?1003` floods input on fast pointers → only on capable terminals; hit test without redraw; existing outbox/backlog bounds fail loudly rather than drop keys; the PTY journey asserts a 200-record motion burst leaves keys intact.
- Kitty image survives a mode change or crash → deletion runs before every screen transition and in the mode-guard drop path; a placement that survives a crash is cosmetic, not state, and the terminal spec already limits restoration promises to modes and cursor.
- Konsole and WezTerm answer the Kitty query partially → ratatui-image blacklists them; Arany accepts only the exact `OK` reply, and the deferred dogfood row lists both emulators; an incorrect positive shows a broken image, never incorrect state.
- The composer header hides chips at narrow widths → no rectangle means no hover; the frame corpus pins it.
- iTerm2 has no delete → the balloon's cells are rewritten on leave (HIDDEN-marked skip cells guarantee a diff), which is iTerm2's documented removal path.
- Sixel needs a decoder → S3 is gated on owner approval of the `png` dependency and may be dropped without affecting S1/S2.

## Vertical slices

1. **S1: Kitty hover preview.** Ships the observable feature end to end on terminals that answer the Kitty query (kitty and Ghostty confirmed by their protocol documentation; Konsole and WezTerm are deferred checks). Input: `KEYBOARD_QUERY` gains the Kitty query; decoder yields `GraphicsReply`; `TerminalQueries` records `GraphicsProtocol::Kitty` and the DA1 parameters; `pointer_modes` adds `?1003` when a protocol exists. Presentation: `draw_frame_with_history` returns chip rectangles; `terminal.rs` hover state with enter/leave transitions, balloon drawing, post-frame Kitty transmit/place (`a=T,f=100,t=d,i=ID,c=,r=,q=2,C=1`) and delete (`a=d,d=I,i=ID,q=2`) on leave, resize, picker/dialog/pager/detail open, chip change, mode switch, release and exit. Spec: all four edits above. Tests: decoder reply table (`OK`, truncated, oversized, foreign APC, DA1 alone), frame corpus (rectangles per width, placement, clamping, shrink, no-fit, skip/HIDDEN cells, repaint after leave), lifecycle tests (move within chip no frame; enter/leave one frame each; disabled mouse, Classic, linear, tmux never probe), byte-exact graphics sequences with the 4096 chunk boundary from the test image padded to multiple chunks, PTY journey with a scripted `OK` reply asserting `?1003h`/`?1003l` and motion burst. Files: `terminal.rs`, `terminal/graphics.rs` (new), `terminal/view.rs`, `terminal/view/tests.rs`, `terminal/input.rs`, `terminal/input/raw.rs`, `tests/session_run/chat_mouse.rs`, `docs/specs/terminal.md`. One PR, roughly 700–900 lines including tests.
2. **S2: iTerm2 inline images.** When no `OK` reply arrives and `TMUX` is unset, `TERM_PROGRAM` in {`iTerm.app`, `WezTerm`} or `LC_TERMINAL=iTerm2` selects `GraphicsProtocol::ITerm2`; emission is one `OSC 1337 File=inline=1;width=C;height=R;preserveAspectRatio=1:BASE64 ST`; leave repaints cells. Spec: the detection sentence and the emulator list. Tests: env table for detection order (Kitty reply wins; tmux suppresses), byte-exact OSC. Files: `terminal/graphics.rs`, `terminal.rs`, spec. One PR, roughly 150–250 lines. Deferred: iTerm2 and WezTerm visual check in NEXT_STEPS.
3. **S3: Sixel.** Gate: owner approval of `png` (image-rs, MIT/Apache-2.0; pulls its inflate crates, `crc32fast` and `bitflags`, all under licenses deny.toml allows; no build script; security pass for a parsing dependency; the exact closure comes from `cargo tree --locked` at approval time). DA1 attribute `4` without a Kitty reply selects `GraphicsProtocol::Sixel`; cell pixel size from `tcgetwinsize`, else `CSI 16 t` appended to the probe; on enter the PNG is decoded in `spawn_blocking` with decoder limits at `MAX_IMAGE_BYTES` and output at `PREVIEW_MAX_PIXELS`, nearest-neighbour scaled, quantized to a fixed 6×7×6 palette (252 colors, no dynamic quantizer) and Sixel-encoded; the result arrives as a terminal event and is dropped if the hover changed; leave repaints cells (Sixel has no delete). Spec: detection and the Sixel bound. Tests: decoder DA1 table, encoder golden on the 1×1 test image and a 2×2 palette image, late-result drop. Files: `terminal/graphics.rs` (or `graphics/sixel.rs` if the encoder passes 80 lines), `Cargo.toml`, `Cargo.lock`, `deny.toml` if needed, spec. One PR, roughly 500–700 lines. Dropping S3 leaves S1/S2 complete; the owner decides at approval time whether a Sixel-only terminal (foot, xterm, mlterm) is in daily use.
